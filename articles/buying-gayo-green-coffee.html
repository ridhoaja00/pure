A Buyer’s Guide to Gayo Green Coffee

Practical questions for roasters and importers looking at Indonesian Gayo Arabica.

Buying green coffee across borders takes more than choosing a favorite origin. If you are considering Aceh Gayo Arabica for your roasting business or distribution network, a clear checklist can help both buyer and supplier work more confidently.

1. Define the coffee you want

Start with your intended use. Are you sourcing a single-origin offering, an espresso component or a house blend? Explain your target flavor, roast style and preferred processing method. A useful inquiry includes estimated volume and expected order schedule.

2. Request a lot-specific specification

Ask for the exact origin and harvest, whether the product is green or roasted, its grade, screen size, moisture measurement, defect count, packaging format and any available traceability information. Confirm which numbers have been tested for the actual lot.

3. Evaluate samples where possible

Before agreeing to a large order, ask whether a representative sample is available. Roasting and cupping the sample can help you judge aroma, flavor, acidity, body and consistency against your business needs.

4. Verify certificates and claims

A geographical indication describes a protected origin and specification. Organic, Fairtrade or other certifications are separate matters. If a particular certification is required, request current, verifiable documents for the relevant supplier and coffee lot.

5. Agree on commercial terms

Discuss quantities, price basis, currency, payment schedule, shipping terms such as applicable Incoterms, delivery timeline, insurance and responsibility for customs paperwork. Each destination has its own import rules. Work with qualified logistics and customs professionals for compliance.

6. Keep communication simple

A complete first email saves time. Mention your company, country, target volume, preferred process, desired specifications and when you hope to receive the coffee.
