Arabica Coffee

Grown in the highlands of Aceh on the northern end of Sumatra, Gayo Arabica carries a unique sense of place. Its sensory expression varies by farm, elevation, variety, processing and roast profile.

CountryIndonesia

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from coffee buyers

Where does Gayo coffee come from?

Gayo Arabica originates from the Gayo Highlands in Aceh Province, Indonesia, including areas of Aceh Tengah, Bener Meriah and Gayo Lues.

Is Gayo coffee always low in acidity?

Not always. Many cups are perceived as smooth and balanced, but acidity and tasting notes vary by lot, processing, roast and brewing method.

Can I request export pricing or samples?

You can submit an inquiry specifying your destination, quantity and requirements. Availability, sample arrangements, pricing and shipping terms must be confirmed directly.

Do you offer certified organic or Grade 1 coffee?

Please request the documentation and specifications for the particular lot. This website does not claim that every coffee offered holds any specific certification or grade.
